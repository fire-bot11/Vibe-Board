Model Organism Explorer

An interactive journey through the cellular worlds of C. elegans and Arabidopsis thaliana.
Explore anatomy in stunning 3D, examine living cells through the microscope, trace life cycles, and discover the experimental techniques that reveal nature's secrets.

🐛
C. elegans
A 1mm transparent roundworm used in developmental biology, neuroscience, and genetics for over 50 years.
🌱
Arabidopsis
The model plant for understanding growth, development, and how plants sense their environment.
What you'll explore:
💠 3D anatomy with interactive hotspots   •   🔬 Real microscopy techniques   •   🔄 Life cycles   •   ⚗️ Lab protocols
